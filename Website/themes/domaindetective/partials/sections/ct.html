Know about a certificate
before attackers use it.

Every publicly trusted certificate is written to Certificate Transparency logs within minutes. We built our own CT ingestion on the same DomainDetective parsing you use here, so new certificates for your domains surface as they are logged: unexpected issuers, lookalike names and forgotten subdomains included.

  • Native ingestion straight from the RFC 6962 logs Chrome and Apple trust, not a third-party search.
  • Scoped to you with domain allow-lists, so you watch your estate rather than the whole internet.
  • Same engine as the CT subdomain tool and Invoke-DDCertificateInventory in PowerShell.
