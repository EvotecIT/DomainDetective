Principles

Your lookups stay yours

No cookies, analytics or accounts. Browser checks talk to public DNS-over-HTTPS resolvers directly, and nothing you look up is stored.

{{ icon_name = "open"; include "icon" }}

Open to the last line

MIT licensed on GitHub. Read how every verdict is reached, file an issue when you disagree, or ship it inside your own product.

{{ icon_name = "document"; include "icon" }}

Evidence, not a score

Every finding shows the record it came from, the RFC behind it and the exact change to make, so you can hand it to whoever owns the DNS.
