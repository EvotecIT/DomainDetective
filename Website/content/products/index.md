## Why It Lives Here

DomainDetective is the higher-level domain analysis toolkit. DnsClientX is the lower-level DNS client that powers resolver workflows underneath it. Keeping both on the same site makes it easier to move from "inspect a domain" to "build the DNS logic yourself" without context switching.
